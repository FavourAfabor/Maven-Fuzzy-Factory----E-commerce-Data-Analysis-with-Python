# Maven-Fuzzy-Factory----E-commerce-Data-Analysis-with-Python
This project analyses e-commerce order data from Maven Fuzzy Factory using Python and Pandas.  
The objective was to explore sales performance, profitability, customer behaviour, product performance, and ordering patterns, and turn the data into practical business insights.  
Tools Used - Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Jupyter Notebook.
# Analysis Covered
Data structure and quality checks
Revenue and COGS analysis
Gross profit and gross profit margin
Yearly sales performance
Product performance
Customer repeat-purchase behaviour
Product mix over time
Hourly order patterns
Data visualisation
# Key Findings
Total revenue was approximately $1.94M.
Total gross profit was approximately $1.22M.
Overall gross profit margin was approximately 62.7%.
2014 recorded the highest revenue and gross profit.
Product 1 generated the largest share of revenue.
Higher-priced products were not necessarily the highest-volume products.
Repeat customers represented a small proportion of customers but generated higher average revenue than the overall customer base.
Order activity varied by hour, with late morning and afternoon periods showing strong ordering activity.
